## Findings that already change the design
1. Invoice trigger is courier booking/handover for prepaid and COD (L-010). Delivery-time COD invoicing is not required by text.
2. Document type depends on seller status (tax invoice / bill of supply / invoice-cum-bill). Single hard-coded "Tax Invoice" is a legal defect (L-012, L-013).
3. Shipping, packing, COD are in value of supply (L-001); allocation method for multi-rate carts is an unresolved, configurable policy (L-005).
4. Goods sellers shipping inter-State need a GSTIN; gate cross-State shipping on a registered status, with Rule 14A as the onboarding path (L-021, L-026).
5. Composition sellers: no tax collection, Bill of Supply, intra-State only, PAN-wide eligibility, forced exit on cap breach or inter-State order (L-029 to L-033).
6. Discount handling must assume the stricter post-supply credit-note rule from 1 Nov 2026 (L-003).
7. Checkout must show every charge before payment; charged total equals displayed total (L-035).
8. Rates are data, and some are conditional on per-unit pre-tax value (apparel Rs 2,500). The engine needs an effective-dated rate table with threshold rules, loaded from the official schedule, never hard-coded (L-037, L-039).
9. Keep ShopPulse out of the money flow (no payouts, no collecting customer payments for sellers) so GST TCS and income-tax TDS operator duties are not triggered (L-028, L-040).
