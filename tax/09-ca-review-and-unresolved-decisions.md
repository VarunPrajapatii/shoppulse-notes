# 09 CA review and unresolved decisions (Stage 5, 2026-10-31)

For the CA appointed before launch. Every item is phrased as a question that can be answered yes/no or with a value. "Built" = what ShopPulse does until the CA answers. "Check" = the exact text to open (01 grade in brackets: P opened, P-old dated compilation, S secondary, U not verified). "If no" = what changes in the design and how big that change is.
**Blocks:** `LAUNCH` = answer before hosting · `FEATURE` = answer before that feature is switched on (the built default is the safe side or the feature stays off) · `LATER` = refinement after launch.
Context the CA needs first: 00-executive-summary.md (1 page), then 03 (rules matrix, tables only). Money flow: every storefront payment settles to the **seller's own** Razorpay account; ShopPulse collects only its subscription fee and has no payout, transfer or commission code (02 §4; 04 I-1).
