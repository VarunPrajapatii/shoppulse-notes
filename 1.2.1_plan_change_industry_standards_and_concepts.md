## Introduction & Problem Statement

### Your Confusion (Very Valid!)

You have several scenarios where users want to change their subscription:

1. **User on ACTIVE Monthly Plan** (15 days remaining)
   - Wants to upgrade to Half-Yearly plan
   - Question: What happens to the 15 days they already paid for?
   - Question: Do they lose that money?

2. **User on ACTIVE Monthly Plan** (20 days remaining, limits almost full)
   - Products: 95/100 used
   - Orders: 980/1000 used
   - Needs Yearly plan (bigger limits) immediately
   - Question: How to switch mid-cycle without losing access?

3. **User has CANCELED subscription** (10 days remaining)
   - Changed their mind, wants to reactivate
   - Question: Do they get charged immediately or at period end?

4. **User on Half-Yearly plan** (60 days remaining)
   - Business slowing down, wants to downgrade to Monthly
   - Question: Do they get refund for unused 60 days?

### The Core Problem

**You've already collected money for services until `currentPeriodEnd`**. When user wants to change plans, you need to:
- Calculate the value of unused time on OLD plan
- Apply that value to NEW plan
- Update Razorpay subscription without losing tracking
- Handle limits, access, and billing correctly

**Your Instinct**: "I don't think I would give refund" - This is actually a VALID industry approach! Many companies use **credit-based** systems instead of refunds.

## Core Concepts: Proration Explained

### What is Proration?

**Proration** = Proportional calculation of value for partial time periods.

**Simple Example**:
```
Monthly Plan: ₹3,500 for 30 days
User pays on Jan 1 → Service until Jan 30

On Jan 15 (15 days into cycle):
- Days used: 15 days
- Days remaining: 15 days
- Value of unused time: ₹3,500 × (15/30) = ₹1,750
```

This ₹1,750 is the **prorated credit** - the value of services they paid for but didn't use.

### Proration Formula

```
Prorated Credit = (Plan Price) × (Days Remaining / Total Days in Cycle)
```

**Real Calculation Examples**:

#### Example 1: Monthly to Half-Yearly Upgrade
```
Current Plan: Monthly (₹3,500/month)
Current Period: May 1 - May 31 (30 days)
Change Date: May 10 (20 days remaining)

Step 1: Calculate unused value
Prorated Credit = ₹3,500 × (20/30) = ₹2,333.33

Step 2: Apply to new plan
New Plan: Half-Yearly (₹18,000 for 180 days)
User pays: ₹18,000 - ₹2,333.33 = ₹15,666.67 immediately

Step 3: New subscription period
Starts: May 10
Ends: November 6 (180 days from May 10)
```

#### Example 2: Half-Yearly to Monthly Downgrade
```
Current Plan: Half-Yearly (₹18,000 for 180 days)
Current Period: Jan 1 - Jun 30 (180 days)
Change Date: Apr 1 (90 days remaining)

Step 1: Calculate unused value
Prorated Credit = ₹18,000 × (90/180) = ₹9,000

Step 2: User has ₹9,000 credit
Option A (Schedule change): Keep half-yearly until Jun 30, then switch to Monthly
Option B (Immediate): Cancel half-yearly, apply ₹9,000 credit to Monthly plan
  - Monthly plan: ₹3,500/month
  - Credit covers: ₹9,000 / ₹3,500 ≈ 2.57 months
  - First charge: After ~2.6 months (when credit exhausted)
```

### The Three Proration Strategies

| Strategy | When Applied | User Pays | Implementation |
|----------|--------------|-----------|----------------|
| **Immediate Proration** | Upgrade to higher-tier plan | **Now**: (New Plan Price - Credit) | Most common for upgrades |
| **Credit Banking** | Downgrade to lower-tier plan | **Later**: Credit applied to future bills | Common for downgrades |
| **Scheduled Change** | Any change, user preference | **At period end**: Full new plan price | Simplest, least refunds |

## Industry Standards: How Big Companies Handle Plan Changes

### 1. **Netflix** 🎬

**Upgrade Flow**:
- **Timing**: Immediate effect
- **Billing**: Prorated charge immediately
- **Example**: Basic (₹199) → Premium (₹649)
  - 15 days into cycle
  - Unused credit: ₹99.50
  - Charged: ₹649 - ₹99.50 = ₹549.50
  - Next billing: Full ₹649 after 30 days

**Downgrade Flow**:
- **Timing**: Scheduled at period end
- **Billing**: No refund, continues on current plan until renewal
- **Reason**: Prevents user confusion, simpler billing

**Key Takeaway**: Asymmetric strategy - Immediate upgrades, scheduled downgrades.

---

### 2. **Spotify** 🎵

**Upgrade Flow**:
- **Timing**: Immediate
- **Billing**: Credit applied, charged difference
- **Example**: Individual (₹119) → Family (₹179)
  - 10 days remaining on Individual
  - Credit: ₹39.67
  - Charged: ₹179 - ₹39.67 = ₹139.33 immediately

**Downgrade Flow**:
- **Timing**: Scheduled at period end
- **Billing**: No partial refunds
- **UI**: Clear message "You'll switch to Premium Individual on [Date]"

**Reactivation**:
- **After Cancel**: User can reactivate anytime before period end
- **Billing**: No charge, just re-enables auto-renewal
- **Access**: Keeps access during canceled period

**Key Takeaway**: User-friendly reactivation, no penalties for changing mind.

---

### 3. **GitHub** 💻

**Upgrade Flow** (Individual → Team):
- **Timing**: Immediate effect
- **Billing**: Prorated to end of current billing cycle
- **Formula**: `(New Plan Price - Old Plan Price) × (Days Remaining / Days in Cycle)`
- **Example**: 
  - Individual: $4/month
  - Team: $4/user/month × 5 users = $20/month
  - 15 days remaining
  - Charged: ($20 - $4) × (15/30) = $8 immediately
  - Next bill: Full $20 after 30 days

**Downgrade Flow**:
- **Timing**: Scheduled at period end
- **Access**: Keeps Team features until cycle ends
- **Data Handling**: If new plan has lower limits, warns user about data loss
- **Example**: Team (5 repos) → Free (3 repos)
  - Warns: "2 repositories will become private"
  - User must choose which repos to keep public

**Key Takeaway**: Handles feature limits gracefully with user choice.

### 4. **Stripe** 💳 (Payment Platform)

Stripe is interesting because they handle plan changes for thousands of SaaS companies. Their default behavior:

**Upgrade (Higher Price)**:
- **Proration**: Automatic
- **Charge**: Immediately bills prorated amount
- **Behavior**: Creates invoice for difference

**Downgrade (Lower Price)**:
- **Proration**: Automatic credit
- **Credit**: Applied as account credit
- **Next Bill**: Reduced by credit amount
- **Credit Expiration**: Never expires (stays on account)

**Example Flow (Account Credit System - Stripe/Shopify)**:
```
Current: $100/month (15 days remaining)
Downgrade to: $50/month

Step 1: Calculate credit
Credit = $100 × (15/30) = $50

Step 2: Apply credit to account & switch immediately
Account credit: $50
New plan starts immediately: $50/month

Step 3: First charge (immediate)
New plan: $50/month
Credit applied: -$50
Charge today: $0 (credit covers it)

Step 4: Next month
Regular $50 charge (credit exhausted)

Note: User switches to $50 plan immediately but first month is free due to credit.
```

**Key Takeaway**: Credit-based system, no refunds, simple accounting.

---

### 5. **Adobe Creative Cloud** 🎨

**Upgrade Flow**:
- **Timing**: Immediate
- **Billing**: Prorated charge
- **Example**: Photography Plan → All Apps
  - Credit applied from old plan
  - Charged difference immediately

**Downgrade Flow**:
- **Timing**: At renewal date
- **Warning**: "You'll lose access to [features] on [date]"
- **Cancel Anytime**: Can cancel scheduled downgrade before it takes effect

**Annual Plans**:
- **Downgrade**: Not allowed mid-year
- **Reason**: Discounted annual pricing locks user in
- **Option**: Must wait until renewal or pay cancellation fee
- **Cancellation Fee**: 50% of remaining contract value

**Key Takeaway**: Annual plans have early termination penalties.

---

### 6. **Zoom** 📹

**Upgrade Flow** (Pro → Business):
- **Timing**: Immediate
- **Billing**: Prorated for current period
- **Seats**: Can add/remove user seats with proration

**Downgrade Flow**:
- **Timing**: Scheduled at renewal
- **Data**: Keeps data (recordings, etc.) but limits access
- **Example**: Business (unlimited cloud) → Pro (1GB cloud)
  - User keeps recordings but can't create new ones after 1GB

**Seat Changes** (Unique Feature):
- Add users mid-cycle: Prorated charge
- Remove users: Credit for remaining days
- Formula: `(Price per seat) × (Days remaining / Cycle days)`

**Key Takeaway**: Per-seat proration for team plans.

---

### 7. **Shopify** 🛍️ (E-commerce SaaS)

**Upgrade Flow**:
- **Timing**: Immediate
- **Billing**: Prorated charge (via app credits system)
- **App Credits**: Shopify's internal credit system
  - Unused time converts to credits
  - Credits auto-apply to new plan charge
  - Example: $29 plan → $79 plan (15 days left)
    - Credit: $14.50
    - Charge today: $79 - $14.50 = $64.50
  - **Same as**: Prorated immediate charge (just different wording)

**Downgrade Flow**:
- **Timing**: Immediate or scheduled (user choice)
- **Feature Lock**: Warns about feature loss
- **Example**: Shopify (250 locations) → Basic (4 locations)
  - Must reduce locations BEFORE downgrading
  - System prevents downgrade if limits exceeded

**Key Takeaway**: Enforces limits BEFORE allowing downgrade.

## The Three Types of Plan Changes

### 1. **Upgrade** (Lower → Higher Tier)

**Definition**: Moving to a plan with:
- Higher price
- Better features
- Higher limits

**Industry Standard Approach**:
- ✅ **Immediate effect** (user needs better service NOW)
- ✅ **Prorated charge** (fair to both parties)
- ✅ **Credit for unused time** on old plan
- ✅ **User pays difference** immediately

**Example**:
```
Monthly (₹3,500) → Half-Yearly (₹18,000)
Days remaining: 20 out of 30
Credit: ₹2,333.33
User pays NOW: ₹18,000 - ₹2,333.33 = ₹15,666.67
New period: Starts today, lasts 180 days
```

**Why Immediate?**
- User is hitting limits (products, orders)
- User wants better features now
- User willing to pay more NOW

---

### 2. **Downgrade** (Higher → Lower Tier)

**Definition**: Moving to a plan with:
- Lower price
- Fewer features
- Lower limits

**Industry Standard Approach**:
- ⏰ **Scheduled at period end** (most common)
- 💰 **No refund** for unused time
- 🎁 **Credit applied** to future bills (alternative)
- ⚠️ **Warning about feature loss**

**Example**:
```
Half-Yearly (₹18,000) → Monthly (₹3,500)
Days remaining: 60 out of 180
Unused value: ₹6,000

Option A (Scheduled Change - RECOMMENDED):
- Continue Half-Yearly until period end (60 days)
- Switch to Monthly at renewal
- No refund, no credits
- User keeps all features for paid period

Option B (Immediate with Credits):
- Cancel Half-Yearly now
- Apply ₹6,000 credit to account
- Start Monthly plan (₹3,500/month)
- First 1.7 months free (credit covers it)
- Regular billing after credit exhausted
```

**Why Scheduled?**
- Simpler accounting
- User already paid for period
- Avoids refund complexity
- User might change mind

---

### 3. **Lateral Move** (Same Tier, Different Cycle)

**Definition**: Changing billing frequency without tier change:
- Monthly → Yearly (same features)
- Yearly → Monthly (same features)

**Industry Standard Approach**:
- 🔄 **Scheduled at period end** (always)
- 💵 **No proration** (different commitment levels)
- 🎯 **Discount retained** on longer cycles

**Example**:
```
Monthly (₹3,500/month) → Yearly (₹38,500/year)
Days remaining: 15 out of 30

Scheduled Change:
- Continue Monthly until period end (15 days)
- Charge ₹38,500 at renewal
- New period: 365 days
- User saves ₹3,500 (annual discount)

Why Not Immediate?
- Different commitment (monthly vs annual)
- Pricing models incompatible for proration
- Would confuse billing cycles
```

## Refund vs Credit vs No-Refund Policies

### Policy Comparison

| Policy | How It Works | Pros | Cons | Best For |
|--------|--------------|------|------|----------|
| **Direct Refund** | Money returned to payment method | User-friendly, builds trust | Complex accounting, payment processor fees, potential abuse | High-value enterprise plans |
| **Account Credit** | Credit stored, applied to future bills | Simple accounting, keeps money in system | User can't cash out | SaaS, subscription businesses |
| **No Refund** | User keeps service until period end | Simplest implementation | Feels less fair to users | Downgrade scenarios |

---

### 1. **Direct Refund Policy**

**How It Works**:
```
User: Monthly plan (₹3,500), 20 days remaining
Action: Downgrades to cheaper plan
Refund: ₹2,333.33 back to credit card

Process:
1. Calculate prorated value
2. Initiate refund via payment gateway
3. Wait 5-7 business days for refund
4. Update subscription immediately
```

**Razorpay Refund Process**:
```javascript
// Partial refund example
const refund = await razorpay.payments.refund(paymentId, {
  amount: 233333, // ₹2,333.33 in paise
  notes: {
    reason: 'Plan downgrade - Unused time refund',
    daysRemaining: 20,
    originalPlan: 'MONTHLY',
    newPlan: 'BASIC'
  }
});

// Refund states: pending → processed (5-7 days)
```

**When to Use**:
- Annual/long-term plan cancellations with significant unused time
- Enterprise customers (B2B)
- Legal requirements (some countries mandate prorated refunds)
- High-value plans (₹50,000+)

**Downsides**:
- Payment gateway fees (Razorpay: 2% + ₹3 per transaction)
  - Refunding ₹2,333 costs you ~₹49 in fees
- Accounting complexity
- Refund fraud risk
- Takes 5-7 days (user impatience)

---

### 2. **Account Credit Policy** ⭐ RECOMMENDED FOR YOUR PROJECT

**How It Works**:
```
User: Half-Yearly plan (₹18,000), 90 days remaining
Action: Downgrades to Monthly
Credit: ₹9,000 stored in account

Future Billing:
Month 1: ₹3,500 - ₹3,500 credit = ₹0 (free)
Month 2: ₹3,500 - ₹3,500 credit = ₹0 (free)
Month 3: ₹3,500 - ₹2,000 credit = ₹1,500 charged
Month 4: ₹3,500 - ₹0 credit = ₹3,500 charged (credit exhausted)
```

**Database Schema Addition**:
```prisma
model User {
  id           String  @id
  accountCredit Decimal @default(0) // ← Add this
  // ... rest of fields
}

model CreditTransaction {
  id        String   @id @default(uuid())
  userId    String
  user      User     @relation(...)
  
  amount    Decimal  // Positive = credit added, Negative = credit used
  type      CreditType // PLAN_CHANGE, REFUND, BONUS, USAGE
  reason    String
  metadata  Json?
  
  balanceBefore Decimal
  balanceAfter  Decimal
  
  createdAt DateTime @default(now())
}

enum CreditType {
  PLAN_DOWNGRADE  // From unused plan time
  PLAN_UPGRADE    // Rare, but possible
  CANCELLATION    // User cancelled
  ADMIN_REFUND    // Manual adjustment
  PROMOTIONAL     // Marketing credit
  USAGE_DEDUCTION // Used for payment
}
```

**Implementation Example**:
```typescript
// Add credit when downgrading
async function applyPlanDowngradeCredit(
  userId: string,
  subscriptionId: string,
  oldPlan: { price: number, billingCycle: string },
  daysRemaining: number
) {
  const totalDays = getBillingCycleDays(oldPlan.billingCycle);
  const creditAmount = oldPlan.price * (daysRemaining / totalDays);
  
  await prisma.$transaction(async (tx) => {
    // Get current balance
    const user = await tx.user.findUnique({
      where: { id: userId },
      select: { accountCredit: true }
    });
    
    const balanceBefore = user!.accountCredit;
    const balanceAfter = balanceBefore + creditAmount;
    
    // Update user credit
    await tx.user.update({
      where: { id: userId },
      data: { accountCredit: balanceAfter }
    });
    
    // Create credit transaction record
    await tx.creditTransaction.create({
      data: {
        userId,
        amount: creditAmount,
        type: 'PLAN_DOWNGRADE',
        reason: `Plan downgrade credit: ${daysRemaining} days remaining`,
        metadata: {
          subscriptionId,
          oldPlanPrice: oldPlan.price,
          daysRemaining,
          totalDays,
          prorationFormula: `${oldPlan.price} × (${daysRemaining}/${totalDays})`
        },
        balanceBefore,
        balanceAfter
      }
    });
    
    // Create subscription history
    await tx.subscriptionHistory.create({
      data: {
        subscriptionId,
        action: 'DOWNGRADED',
        fromStatus: 'ACTIVE',
        toStatus: 'ACTIVE',
        reason: `Applied ₹${creditAmount.toFixed(2)} credit for unused time`,
        metadata: {
          creditAmount,
          daysRemaining,
          appliedToAccount: true
        }
      }
    });
  });
  
  return {
    creditAmount,
    newBalance: user!.accountCredit + creditAmount
  };
}

// Use credit during next charge
async function chargeWithCreditApplication(
  userId: string,
  subscriptionId: string,
  planPrice: number
) {
  const user = await prisma.user.findUnique({
    where: { id: userId },
    select: { accountCredit: true }
  });
  
  const creditAvailable = user!.accountCredit;
  const creditToUse = Math.min(creditAvailable, planPrice);
  const amountToCharge = planPrice - creditToUse;
  
  if (amountToCharge > 0) {
    // Charge via Razorpay
    const payment = await createRazorpayCharge(userId, amountToCharge);
  }
  
  // Deduct used credit
  await prisma.$transaction(async (tx) => {
    await tx.user.update({
      where: { id: userId },
      data: { accountCredit: creditAvailable - creditToUse }
    });
    
    await tx.creditTransaction.create({
      data: {
        userId,
        amount: -creditToUse, // Negative = usage
        type: 'USAGE_DEDUCTION',
        reason: 'Credit applied to subscription charge',
        metadata: {
          subscriptionId,
          originalCharge: planPrice,
          creditUsed: creditToUse,
          actualCharge: amountToCharge
        },
        balanceBefore: creditAvailable,
        balanceAfter: creditAvailable - creditToUse
      }
    });
  });
  
  return {
    planPrice,
    creditUsed: creditToUse,
    charged: amountToCharge,
    remainingCredit: creditAvailable - creditToUse
  };
}
```

- ✅ Simple accounting
- ✅ Instant (no waiting for refunds)
- ✅ Encourages continued usage
- ✅ Can use for upgrades, add-ons, etc.

### 3. **No Refund Policy** (Service Until Period End)

**How It Works**:
```
User: Yearly plan (₹38,500), 100 days remaining
Action: Downgrades to Monthly
Result: Keeps Yearly features until period end

Timeline:
Today: Request downgrade → Cancel Razorpay subscription (prevent auto-renewal)
       Store intended new plan in metadata
       User keeps Yearly features

Day 100: Period ends → Subscription expires
         User sees banner: "Activate your Monthly plan now"
         User must manually subscribe to Monthly plan (Razorpay requires authentication)
         
After User Activates: First charge ₹3,500 (monthly rate)

Note: Cannot automatically create new Razorpay subscription in cron job.
User authentication required for payment method approval.
```

**Implementation**:
```typescript
async function scheduleDowngrade(
  subscriptionId: string,
  newPlanId: string,
  currentPeriodEnd: Date
) {
  const subscription = await prisma.subscription.findUnique({
    where: { id: subscriptionId }
  });
  
  // STEP 1: Cancel Razorpay subscription IMMEDIATELY (prevent auto-renewal at period end)
  if (subscription.razorpaySubscriptionId) {
    await razorpay.subscriptions.cancel(
      subscription.razorpaySubscriptionId,
      { cancel_at_cycle_end: true } // Keeps active until period end
    );
  }
  
  // STEP 2: Store scheduled change and update status
  await prisma.subscription.update({
    where: { id: subscriptionId },
    data: {
      status: 'CANCELED', // Mark as canceled
      canceledAt: new Date(),
      cancelAtPeriodEnd: true,
      autoRenew: false,
      metadata: {
        scheduledChange: {
          newPlanId,
          effectiveDate: currentPeriodEnd,
          scheduledAt: new Date(),
          type: 'DOWNGRADE',
          requiresUserReactivation: true
        }
      }
    }
  });
  
  // Cron job will expire subscription and prompt user to reactivate
}

// Cron job: Process expired subscriptions with scheduled changes
async function processScheduledChanges() {
  const now = new Date();
  
  const subscriptionsWithScheduledChanges = await prisma.subscription.findMany({
    where: {
      currentPeriodEnd: { lte: now },
      status: 'CANCELED',
      metadata: {
        path: ['scheduledChange', 'type'],
        equals: 'DOWNGRADE'
      }
    }
  });
  
  for (const subscription of subscriptionsWithScheduledChanges) {
    const { newPlanId } = subscription.metadata.scheduledChange;
    
    // Mark as expired and flag for user action
    await prisma.subscription.update({
      where: { id: subscription.id },
      data: {
        status: 'EXPIRED',
        metadata: {
          scheduledChange: null, // Clear
          downgradePending: true,
          intendedPlanId: newPlanId
        }
      }
    });
    
    // User will see banner: "Your plan expired. Activate Monthly plan now"
    // User clicks button → Opens Razorpay checkout → Authenticates new subscription
    // Cannot automatically create Razorpay subscription (requires user authentication)
  }
}
```

**Benefits**:
- ✅ Simplest implementation
- ✅ Zero refund complexity
- ✅ User gets what they paid for
- ✅ No accounting headaches
- ✅ User might change mind

**Drawbacks**:
- ⚠️ User can't access new plan features immediately
- ⚠️ If downgrading due to cost, user waits for savings
- ⚠️ Might feel "stuck" in expensive plan

**Best For**:
- Downgrades only
- Plans with significant unused time
- When immediate change isn't critical

## Billing Cycle Considerations

### Monthly Plans (30 days)

**Flexibility**: HIGH
- Easy to calculate proration (₹116.67 per day)
- Small credit amounts (max ₹3,500)
- Short wait for scheduled changes (max 30 days)

**Recommendation**:
- **Upgrades**: Immediate with proration
- **Downgrades**: Either immediate or scheduled (user choice)

---

### Half-Yearly Plans (180 days)

**Flexibility**: MEDIUM
- Moderate proration complexity
- Significant credit amounts (up to ₹18,000)
- Longer wait for scheduled changes (up to 180 days)

**Recommendation**:
- **Upgrades**: Immediate with proration
- **Downgrades**: Scheduled preferred (avoid large refunds)
- **Alternative**: Account credit for immediate downgrade

---

### Yearly Plans (365 days)

**Flexibility**: LOW
- Complex proration (₹105.48 per day)
- Very large credit amounts (up to ₹38,500)
- Very long wait (up to 365 days)

**Recommendation**:
- **Upgrades**: Immediate with proration
- **Downgrades**: 
  - Scheduled only (industry standard)
  - OR require early termination fee (50% of remaining)
  - Reason: Annual plans have discounts, designed for commitment

**Early Termination Option**:
```
User: Yearly plan (₹38,500), 200 days remaining
Unused value: ₹21,095
Early termination fee: 50% × ₹21,095 = ₹10,547.50

Options:
1. Wait 200 days, switch to Monthly (no fee)
2. Pay ₹10,547.50 fee, switch immediately with ₹10,547.50 credit

Credit after fee: ₹21,095 - ₹10,547.50 = ₹10,547.50
Months covered: ₹10,547.50 / ₹3,500 = 3 months free
```

### 3. **Handle Limit Overages Gracefully**

```typescript
async function checkDowngradeEligibility(
  userId: string,
  newPlanId: string
) {
  // Get current usage
  const usage = await getCurrentUsage(userId);
  
  // Get new plan limits
  const newPlan = await prisma.subscriptionPlan.findUnique({
    where: { id: newPlanId }
  });
  
  const overages = [];
  
  if (usage.products > newPlan.maxProducts) {
    overages.push({
      metric: 'Products',
      current: usage.products,
      limit: newPlan.maxProducts,
      excess: usage.products - newPlan.maxProducts,
      action: 'DELETE_OR_ARCHIVE'
    });
  }
  
  if (usage.storageGB > newPlan.maxStorageGB) {
    overages.push({
      metric: 'Storage',
      current: usage.storageGB,
      limit: newPlan.maxStorageGB,
      excess: usage.storageGB - newPlan.maxStorageGB,
      action: 'DELETE_FILES'
    });
  }
  
  return {
    canDowngrade: overages.length === 0,
    overages
  };
}
```

## Immediate vs Scheduled Changes

### Decision Matrix

| Scenario | Immediate | Scheduled | Recommended |
|----------|-----------|-----------|-------------|
| **Upgrade (any cycle)** | ✅ Prorated charge | ⚠️ User waits for features | **Immediate** |
| **Downgrade (Monthly)** | ✅ Small credit | ✅ Simple | **User choice** |
| **Downgrade (Half-Yearly)** | ⚠️ Large credit/refund | ✅ Clean | **Scheduled** |
| **Downgrade (Yearly)** | ❌ Very large refund | ✅ Industry standard | **Scheduled only** |
| **Lateral (change cycle)** | ❌ Complex | ✅ Clean transition | **Scheduled** |
| **Reactivation (CANCELED)** | ✅ User changed mind | N/A | **Immediate** |
| **Usage overage urgent** | ✅ Need limits now | ❌ Might hit limits | **Immediate** |

### Immediate Change Implementation Pattern

```typescript
async function immediateUpgrade(
  userId: string,
  subscriptionId: string,
  newPlanId: string
) {
  const subscription = await prisma.subscription.findUnique({
    where: { id: subscriptionId },
    include: { plan: true }
  });
  
  const newPlan = await prisma.subscriptionPlan.findUnique({
    where: { id: newPlanId }
  });
  
  // Step 1: Calculate proration
  const now = new Date();
  const daysRemaining = differenceInDays(
    subscription.currentPeriodEnd,
    now
  );
  const totalDays = getBillingCycleDays(subscription.plan.billingCycle);
  const credit = Number(subscription.plan.price) * (daysRemaining / totalDays);
  
  // Step 2: Calculate charge (for display purposes)
  const newPlanPrice = Number(newPlan.price);
  const chargeAmount = newPlanPrice - credit;
  
  // Step 3: Create new Razorpay subscription with proration addon
  // NOTE: Do NOT cancel old subscription yet!
  const newRazorpaySub = await razorpay.subscriptions.create({
    plan_id: newPlan.razorpayPlanId,
    customer_id: subscription.razorpayCustomerId,
    total_count: getCountForBillingCycle(newPlan.billingCycle),
    start_at: Math.floor(now.getTime() / 1000),
    quantity: 1,
    
    // Apply proration credit as negative addon
    addons: [
      {
        item: {
          name: "Upgrade Proration Credit",
          amount: Math.round(credit * -100), // Negative in paise
          currency: "INR",
          description: `${daysRemaining} days unused on ${subscription.plan.displayName}`
        }
      }
    ],
    
    customer_notify: 1,
    notes: {
      userId,
      oldSubscriptionId: subscription.razorpaySubscriptionId,
      replacingSubscription: 'true',
      prorationCredit: credit.toFixed(2)
    }
  });
  
  // Step 4: Return checkout URL (payment happens in webhook)
  return {
    checkoutUrl: newRazorpaySub.short_url,
    credit,
    chargeAmount,
    status: 'awaiting_payment'
  };
  
  // Step 5: In webhook (after payment succeeds):
  // Cancel old Razorpay subscription
  if (subscription.razorpaySubscriptionId) {
    await razorpay.subscriptions.cancel(
      subscription.razorpaySubscriptionId,
      { cancel_at_cycle_end: false }
    );
  }
  
  // Update database (this happens in webhook handler)
  // Database update happens in webhook handler after payment succeeds
  await tx.subscription.update({
    where: { id: subscriptionId },
    data: {
      planId: newPlanId,
      razorpaySubscriptionId: newRazorpaySub.id,
      razorpayPlanId: newPlan.razorpayPlanId,
      currentPeriodStart: now,
      currentPeriodEnd: addDays(now, getBillingCycleDays(newPlan.billingCycle)),
      status: 'ACTIVE',
      updatedAt: now
    }
  });
  
  // Create history
  await tx.subscriptionHistory.create({
    data: {
      subscriptionId,
      action: 'UPGRADED',
      fromStatus: subscription.status,
      toStatus: 'ACTIVE',
      fromPlanId: subscription.planId,
      toPlanId: newPlanId,
      reason: `Upgraded from ${subscription.plan.displayName} to ${newPlan.displayName}`,
      metadata: {
        upgradeType: 'immediate_with_proration',
        prorationCredit: credit,
        firstChargeAmount: chargeAmount,
        daysRemaining,
        oldRazorpaySubId: subscription.razorpaySubscriptionId,
        newRazorpaySubId: newRazorpaySub.id
      }
    }
  });
}
```

```typescript
async function scheduleDowngrade(
  userId: string,
  subscriptionId: string,
  newPlanId: string
) {
  const subscription = await prisma.subscription.findUnique({
    where: { id: subscriptionId },
    include: { plan: true }
  });
  
  const newPlan = await prisma.subscriptionPlan.findUnique({
    where: { id: newPlanId }
  });
  
  // Check eligibility (usage within new plan limits)
  const eligibility = await checkDowngradeEligibility(userId, newPlanId);
  if (!eligibility.canDowngrade) {
    throw new Error('Usage exceeds new plan limits');
  }
  
  // CRITICAL: Cancel Razorpay subscription IMMEDIATELY to prevent auto-renewal
  // This prevents charging the user for the old plan at period end
  if (subscription.razorpaySubscriptionId) {
    await razorpay.subscriptions.cancel(
      subscription.razorpaySubscriptionId,
      { cancel_at_cycle_end: true } // Keeps subscription active until period end
    );
  }
  
  // Store scheduled change in subscription metadata
  await prisma.subscription.update({
    where: { id: subscriptionId },
    data: {
      status: 'CANCELED', // Mark as canceled
      canceledAt: new Date(),
      cancelAtPeriodEnd: true,
      autoRenew: false, // Disable autopay
      metadata: {
        scheduledChange: {
          type: 'DOWNGRADE',
          newPlanId,
          newPlanName: newPlan.displayName,
          effectiveDate: subscription.currentPeriodEnd,
          scheduledAt: new Date(),
          scheduledBy: userId,
          razorpayCanceled: true
        }
      }
    }
  });
  
  // Create history record
  await prisma.subscriptionHistory.create({
    data: {
      subscriptionId,
      action: 'DOWNGRADED', // Future action
      fromStatus: subscription.status,
      toStatus: subscription.status, // No immediate status change
      fromPlanId: subscription.planId,
      toPlanId: newPlanId,
      reason: `Scheduled downgrade from ${subscription.plan.displayName} to ${newPlan.displayName}`,
      metadata: {
        effectiveDate: subscription.currentPeriodEnd,
        daysUntilChange: differenceInDays(
          subscription.currentPeriodEnd,
          new Date()
        ),
        willTakeEffect: subscription.currentPeriodEnd
      }
    }
  });
  
  return {
    effectiveDate: subscription.currentPeriodEnd,
    daysUntilChange: differenceInDays(
      subscription.currentPeriodEnd,
      new Date()
    )
  };
}

// Cron job to apply scheduled changes
// IMPORTANT: Cannot automatically create new Razorpay subscription (requires user authentication)
async function applyScheduledChanges() {
  const now = new Date();
  
  // Find subscriptions with scheduled changes where period has ended
  const subscriptions = await prisma.subscription.findMany({
    where: {
      currentPeriodEnd: { lte: now },
      metadata: {
        path: ['scheduledChange', 'type'],
        not: null
      }
    },
    include: { plan: true }
  });
  
  for (const subscription of subscriptions) {
    const { newPlanId, type } = subscription.metadata.scheduledChange;
    
    try {
      // Mark subscription as expired and flag for manual reactivation
      await prisma.subscription.update({
        where: { id: subscription.id },
        data: {
          status: 'EXPIRED',
          metadata: {
            scheduledChange: null, // Clear scheduled change
            downgradePending: true, // Flag for UI
            intendedPlanId: newPlanId,
            requiresUserAction: true
          }
        }
      });
      
      // Create history
      await prisma.subscriptionHistory.create({
        data: {
          subscriptionId: subscription.id,
          action: 'EXPIRED',
          fromStatus: 'ACTIVE',
          toStatus: 'EXPIRED',
          reason: `Period ended. User must manually activate ${type === 'DOWNGRADE' ? 'downgraded' : 'new'} plan.`,
          metadata: {
            scheduledChangeApplied: true,
            awaitingUserReactivation: true,
            intendedPlanId: newPlanId
          }
        }
      });
      
      console.log(`✅ Marked subscription ${subscription.id} as expired. User must reactivate.`);
      // TODO: Send email notification to user
    } catch (error) {
      console.error(`❌ Failed to process scheduled change:`, error);
      // TODO: Send alert to admin
    }
  }
}
```

## Summary: Key Takeaways

### 1. **Your Instinct Was Right**
- "I don't think I would give refund" → ✅ Correct!
- Most SaaS companies use **credit-based** or **no-refund** policies
- Refunds are rare, complex, and costly

### 2. **The Two Strategies**
- **Upgrades**: Immediate + Proration (industry standard)
- **Downgrades**: Scheduled at period end (simplest) OR Account credit (best UX)

### 3. **Recommended for Your Project**
```
Upgrade Flow:
├─ Calculate unused credit from old plan
├─ Charge (new plan - credit) immediately
├─ Cancel old Razorpay subscription
├─ Create new Razorpay subscription
└─ User gets immediate access to new limits

Downgrade Flow (Option A - Simplest):
├─ Schedule change for currentPeriodEnd
├─ User keeps current plan features
├─ Auto-switch at renewal
└─ No credits, no refunds, no complexity

Downgrade Flow (Option B - Better UX):
├─ Calculate unused credit
├─ Add to user.accountCredit
├─ Cancel old plan, start new plan
├─ Auto-apply credit to future bills
└─ User pays reduced amount (or free) for X months
```

### 4. **What Razorpay Offers** (Preview for Part 2)
- ✅ Subscription updates
- ✅ Addon charges (one-time charges for proration)
- ✅ Pause/resume
- ✅ Cancel with `cancel_at_cycle_end` option
- ❌ No built-in proration (you calculate)
- ❌ No built-in credit system (you implement)
- ❌ **Cannot auto-create subscriptions** (requires user authentication for payment approval)
- ⚠️ **Schedule changes** may not support plan_id changes (check docs)
